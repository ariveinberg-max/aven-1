## Failed (found and fixed)

- **Checkpoint backup silently failing** — first a macOS Full Disk Access gap, then a separate iCloud file-lock race on the tokenizer.json copy specifically. Both found and fixed; verified through the actual production launchd path, not just manually.
- **RAFT collapsed the preference-labeling tool's diversity** — 29 of 35 pair-generation calls came back byte-identical after RAFT made the model too confident on the affected prompts. Fixed in two rounds (prompt swap + temperature change, then server-side retry with a plausibility filter); verified live.
- **Cross-machine checkpoint loading broken** — a torch-version gap meant `evaluate_capabilities.py` couldn't load Aven-2's checkpoint at all on the Windows PC. Fixed with a targeted numpy safe-globals allowlist.
- **Aven-2 training fully stopped** — a related but distinct version of the same torch-version gap crash-looped `train.py` itself through its entire 20-retry budget and stopped training outright, silently, until caught by coincidence. Fixed, redeployed, and confirmed genuinely recovered (not just no-longer-erroring).
- **Research summarizer hallucinated a number** — invented a perplexity value for Aven-1 that was never in its actual input, copying it from Aven-2's real number in the same prompt. Documented as a clean example of "noisy triage aid, not ground truth."
- **First public X post got near-zero engagement** — expected outcome for a brand-new account's first post, not a real failure; addressed by adding a real weekly (not daily) posting cadence to the roadmap instead of chasing a better single post.
