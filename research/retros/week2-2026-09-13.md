# Week 2 retrospective (Days 8–13)

## Shipped

- Confirmed the checkpoint-backup fix from Week 1 through the real production launchd path (not just a manual test).
- Built `kto.py` — the third real RLHF method, with a genuine mismatched-pair reference-point (z0) estimate, and 5 real tests.
- Ran a second reward-model + RAFT cycle on the expanded dataset (162 usable pairs), promoted after live verification.

- Four real, substantive reading-block findings, each directly tied to something observed the same day: PyTorch's `weights_only` mechanism, KTO's theoretical foundation, why PPO needs more data than DPO, why reward-model accuracy plateaus with noisy labels, and mode collapse from RLHF fine-tuning.

## Failed (found and fixed)

- **KTO's first draft had a real correctness bug**, caught in review before it ever ran: the reference-point (z0) calculation reordered already-computed rewards instead of scoring genuinely mismatched prompt/response combinations, which would have made the whole mechanism meaningless. Fixed with real mismatched-pair forward passes before the first run.
- **The reward model got *worse* with more data**: 75% → 60.53% held-out accuracy despite nearly double the comparisons. Reported plainly, not smoothed over — later explained by the Day 12 reading on noisy-label plateaus.
- **RAFT's candidate diversity collapsed independently of the labeling-tool fix**: `raft.py` has its own separate temperature schedule that never received the diversity fix applied to `server.py` earlier — same underlying phenomenon (later named precisely by the Day 13 mode-collapse reading), hitting a second, independent piece of code. Still not fixed.
- **PPO produced a real, live-verified regression** ("Thank you" → incoherent text) that the frozen capability suite completely missed (0 regressions shown) — concrete, repeated evidence for why live-checking matters every time, not just as a formality.
- **A shared metadata bug across three scripts**: none of `dpo.py`/`kto.py`/`ppo.py`'s saved checkpoints included a `step` field, so `evaluate_capabilities.py` crashed trying to evaluate them — meaning neither the DPO nor KTO checkpoints from this session had ever actually been run through the full frozen-suite eval, only spot-checked live. Fixed all three; patched the already-saved files in place.
